Quick Answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about joining NEXORA and participating in club programs.

How do I become a member of NEXORA?

You can register via the 'JOIN US' button on this portal or attend our introductory orientation session at the start of each semester. Membership is open to all students!

Do I need prior coding experience?

Not at all! NEXORA offers dedicated beginner tracks in web development, Python, and UI/UX design. We start from the absolute fundamentals.

Are NEXORA workshops and hackathons free?

Yes, all internal bootcamps, workshops, tech talks, and open-source hackathons organized by NEXORA are completely free for registered college students.

Can non-CSE students join the club?

Absolutely! We encourage students from IT, ECE, Mechanical, and all departments who have an interest in technology and software to join.
